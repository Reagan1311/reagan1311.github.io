Gen Li

Full Professor @ HNU

Email: ligen [at] g.skku.edu

I am a incoming Professor at the School of AI and Robotics, Hunan University. My research focuses on building reliable, efficient, and human-centered embodied intelligence that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. I am broadly interested in robot learning, multimodal foundation models, efficient AI, and human-robot interaction.

I was a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, working with Jianfei Yang. I received my PhD in Robotics and Autonomous Systems from The University of Edinburgh, UK, supervised by Laura Sevilla and co-supervised by Timothy Hospedales.

📢 I am always looking for highly motivated PhD and master’s students, postdocs, research assistants/interns, and visiting scholars. Please feel free to reach out by email.
